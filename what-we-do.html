What We Do

Workshops & Events

We host regular workshops and events where you can learn new skills, work on projects, and connect with other members of our community. From coding bootcamps to hackathons, there's always something exciting happening at Neighborhood Devs!

Mentorship & Support

Our experienced mentors are here to support you every step of the way. Whether you need help with a project, want feedback on your code, or just want to chat about your ideas, we're here for you!

Community Projects

We encourage our members to collaborate on community projects that solve real-world problems. It's a great opportunity to apply your skills, work with others, and make a positive impact in our neighborhood!
