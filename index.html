Neighborhood Devs

Welcome to Neighborhood Devs!

Hey there! We're so excited to have you here. At Neighborhood Devs, we're all about creating a fun and supportive space for young coders and makers to learn, create, and connect. Whether you're just starting out or already a coding pro, there's something for everyone here!

Our mission is to empower you with the skills and confidence to build amazing things with code. We offer a variety of resources, workshops, and projects that are designed to be fun, engaging, and educational. From coding challenges to community events, there's always something exciting happening at Neighborhood Devs!

So dive in, explore our resources, join our community events, and let's build something awesome together! Remember, the only limit is your imagination. Happy coding!
